# Overview

<PlanRequirement plan="Solo" />

**Sold packages** are packages already issued to customers. Each row is one customer purchase with remaining and used credits, status, and purchase or expiry dates.

Open **Appointments → Catalog → Sold packages** in the side menu.

Catalog definitions live under [Packages](/docs/catalog/packages). Public booking availability is under [Services and packages](/docs/daily-use/appointment-settings-services-packages).

## What you can do here

- [Listing sold packages](./sold-packages/listing-sold-packages) - search and filter customer purchases.
- [Working with sold packages](./sold-packages/working-with-sold-packages) - schedule, view appointments or the customer, adjust used credits, cancel, or reactivate.

### Good to know

Statuses include **Pending payment**, **Active**, **Exhausted**, **Expired**, and **Cancelled**.
