# Creating a new gift card

<PlanRequirement plan="Solo" />

Issue a gift card when a client buys prepaid credit they can spend on bookings. You record payment in person (**Cash** or **Card**) or send a **Payment link** so they pay online. After save, the code is fixed; you cannot change it later.

## Before you start

- Know the **Amount** and whether the card should expire.
- Have the **Customer** in your client list.
- For a **Payment link**, install a payment app under **Apps** (for example Stripe, Square, or PayPal). **Payment link** only appears in the payment method list when at least one such app can send a link.

## Create the gift card

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Gift cards**.
2. Click **Add New**.

The **New gift card** form opens.

![New gift card form](/img/catalog/gift-cards/create-form.png)

## Gift card code

1. Keep the generated **Code**, or click the dice / regenerate control for another unique code.
2. Optionally copy the code with the copy control so you can send it outside Hacado.

Rules:

- Required, unique in the workspace.
- Length is limited (typically a short segmented code such as `27D-51C-BJJ`).
- You **cannot** edit the code after the gift card is saved.

## Amount and expiry

1. Enter **Amount** - the prepaid balance the customer can spend (currency). Must be greater than zero.
2. Optionally set **Expires at**. Leave empty for no expiry. After that date the card cannot be used for new payments even if balance remains.

Example: **$100** with expiry **31 Dec 2027** → client can redeem until that datetime, up to $100 of bookings.

## Customer and payment

1. Pick the **Customer** who bought the card. Required. Credits and history stay tied to that customer.
2. Choose **Payment method**:

![Payment method Cash or Card](/img/catalog/gift-cards/payment-method.png)

| Method | When to use | What happens on Save |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash** | Paid in person with cash | Gift card is created and treated as paid. |
| **Card** | Paid in person by card (terminal / offline) | Same as cash: created as paid in Catalog. |
| **Payment link** | Customer will pay online | Appears only when a payment-link app is available. Card stays pending until the link is paid (or cancelled). |

### Payment link

![Payment link options](/img/catalog/gift-cards/payment-link.png)

1. Choose **Payment link** as the payment method.
2. If more than one payment app is installed, pick the **App**.
3. Set **Send via**:
   - **Email** - send the link to a customer email destination.
   - **SMS** - send by text (requires SMS setup and consent when Hacado asks).
   - **Copy link** - Hacado copies the URL after create so you can paste it yourself.
4. For **Email** or **SMS**, pick the **Destination** from the customer’s known contacts.
5. Click **Save**.

### In-person payment

1. Choose **Cash** or **Card**.
2. Click **Save**.

Hacado records the in-store payment and creates the gift card immediately.

## Save

1. Click **Save**.

Hacado opens the gift card (or confirms create). For **Copy link**, copy the URL from the confirmation when shown.

### Good to know

- Configure a default payment app under **Apps → Default apps** if create fails with a payment-app error.
- Activating or deactivating later is covered in [Activating and deactivating](./activating-and-deactivating).
- Delete rules (when delete is blocked) are in [Deleting a gift card](./deleting-a-gift-card).
