# Updating an existing gift card

Change a gift card when the expiry date needs updating, or when a pending payment-link amount still needs a correction. **Code** and **Customer** are locked after create.

## Open the gift card

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Gift cards**.
2. Click the gift card code, or open the row menu and choose **Update**.

![Gift cards list](/img/catalog/gift-cards/listing.png)

## What you can change

![Edit form with locked fields](/img/catalog/gift-cards/edit-locked.png)

1. **Code** stays locked. You cannot regenerate it after create.
2. **Customer** stays locked.
3. Update **Expires at** when the redemption window should change, or clear it for no expiry.
4. Update **Amount** only when the card’s payment is still a pending **Payment link**. For paid cards and in-person payments, amount is locked.

Full create-time detail for each field is in [Creating a new gift card](./creating-a-new-gift-card).

## Save

1. Click **Save**.

Hacado keeps you on the gift card page and confirms that your changes were saved.

### Good to know

To stop redemption without deleting the card, use [Activate](./activating-and-deactivating) / **Deactivate** instead of editing amount.
