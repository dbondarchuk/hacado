# Listing gift cards

The **Gift cards** list shows every gift card in your catalog. Use it to find a card, open one to edit, activate or deactivate it, or delete when Hacado allows.

Open **Appointments → Catalog → Gift cards**.

![Gift cards list](/img/catalog/gift-cards/listing.png)

## Add a gift card

1. Click **Add New**.

See [Creating a new gift card](./creating-a-new-gift-card).

## Search and filters

1. Click the search box above the table.
2. Type part of the gift card code.
3. Optionally filter by status, customer, or expiry date range.
4. Click **Reset filters** when you want every gift card again.

## Row menu

1. Open the menu on a gift card row.
2. Choose **Update**, **Activate** or **Deactivate**, or **Delete** when available.

![Row menu on a gift card](/img/catalog/gift-cards/row-menu.png)

- **Update** opens the gift card for editing. You can also click the code.
- **Activate** / **Deactivate** change whether the card can be redeemed. See [Activating and deactivating](./activating-and-deactivating).
- **Delete** appears only when the card can be removed. See [Deleting a gift card](./deleting-a-gift-card).

## Activate or deactivate several gift cards

1. Check the rows you want to change, or use **Select all**.
2. Click **Activate** or **Deactivate** for the selection.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

## Delete several gift cards

1. Check the rows you want to remove, or use **Select all**.
2. Click **Delete selected** when it is available.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

Only gift cards that are allowed to be deleted can be removed in bulk. Cards that are in use, paid online, or owned by an app stay in the list.

### Good to know

If **Delete** is missing or disabled on a row, open [Deleting a gift card](./deleting-a-gift-card) for the rules.
