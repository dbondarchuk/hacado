# Deleting a gift card

Delete a gift card only when Hacado allows it. Many cards must stay for payment history.

## When delete is allowed

You can delete a gift card when all of these are true:

- It is not **in use** (no payments have been made with the card).
- It was not paid with an **Online** method.
- It does not have a **Payment link** that is already **Paid**.
- It is not **app-owned** (created by an app such as Gift Card Studio). App-owned cards show a message that they can be deleted only from that app.

When delete is blocked, **Delete** is hidden, disabled, or replaced with a message that names the source app.

![Delete disabled or unavailable](/img/catalog/gift-cards/delete-disabled.png)

## Delete one gift card

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Gift cards**.
2. Open the row menu on the gift card.
3. Click **Delete** when it is available.

![Row menu](/img/catalog/gift-cards/row-menu.png)

4. Confirm in the dialog, or cancel to keep the card.

## Delete several gift cards

1. Check only cards that are allowed to be deleted.
2. Click **Delete selected**.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

## What happens next

- The gift card leaves the **Gift cards** list.
- You cannot undo a delete. Issue a new card if the client still needs credit - see [Creating a new gift card](./creating-a-new-gift-card).

### Good to know

If you only need to stop redemption, [deactivate](./activating-and-deactivating) the card instead of deleting it.
