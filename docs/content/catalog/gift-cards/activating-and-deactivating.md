# Activating and deactivating

Use **Activate** and **Deactivate** to control whether a gift card can be redeemed. Deactivating does not delete the card or change its remaining amount.

## One gift card

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Gift cards**.
2. Open the row menu on the gift card.

![Row menu with Activate or Deactivate](/img/catalog/gift-cards/row-menu.png)

3. Click **Deactivate** when the card is **Active**.
4. Click **Activate** when the card is inactive and activation is allowed.

**Activate** is not offered while a payment-link payment is still **Pending** or **Cancelled**. Finish or replace that payment first, then try again.

## Several gift cards

1. Check the rows to change, or use **Select all**.
2. Click **Activate** or **Deactivate** for the selection.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

## What happens next

- An **Active** card can be applied to payments while it has amount left and has not expired.
- An **Inactive** card stays in the list but cannot be redeemed until you activate it again.

### Good to know

Expired cards and cards with no amount left show their own status badges. Activating does not extend an expiry date - edit **Expires at** on the gift card if needed.
