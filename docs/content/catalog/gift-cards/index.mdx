# Overview

<PlanRequirement plan="Solo" />

**Gift cards** are prepaid credit clients can spend on bookings. Each card has a unique **Code**, an **Amount**, optional expiry, a **Customer**, and a payment record. You can activate or deactivate cards, and send a payment link when the client pays online.

Open **Appointments → Catalog → Gift cards** in the side menu.

For designing and selling gift cards on your website, see the Gift Card Studio app under **Apps**.

## What you can do here

- [Listing gift cards](./gift-cards/listing-gift-cards) - search, filter, open a row, or delete when allowed.
- [Creating a new gift card](./gift-cards/creating-a-new-gift-card) - issue a card with in-person payment or a payment link.
- [Updating an existing gift card](./gift-cards/updating-an-existing-gift-card) - change expiry, and amount when still pending.
- [Activating and deactivating](./gift-cards/activating-and-deactivating) - turn redeemability on or off.
- [Deleting a gift card](./gift-cards/deleting-a-gift-card) - remove a card when delete is allowed.

### Good to know

Delete is blocked when the card is already in use, was paid online (including a paid payment link), or was created by an app. See [Deleting a gift card](./gift-cards/deleting-a-gift-card).
