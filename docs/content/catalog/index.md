# Catalog

**Catalog** is where you define what clients can book or buy: services, extras, questions on the booking form, discounts, gift cards, and packages.

Open **Appointments → Catalog** in the side menu.

## Definitions vs booking availability

Items you create in Catalog are definitions. Creating a service or package does not by itself put it on your public booking page. For services, Hacado may ask after you save whether to add the service to booking availability. You can also choose what is bookable later under **Settings → Appointments → Services and packages**.

See [Services and packages (coming soon)](/docs/daily-use/appointment-settings-services-packages).

## Where to start

If you are still finishing first-time setup, begin with [Set up your services](/docs/first-steps/set-up-your-services), then come back here for fields, add-ons, staff, payments rules, and Solo features.

### Good to know

- Catalog changes apply to new bookings. Existing appointments keep what they already stored.
- Team staff assignment and member overrides need more than one active member (Studio seats).
