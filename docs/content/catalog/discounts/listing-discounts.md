# Listing discounts

The **Discounts** list shows every discount in your catalog. Use it to find a discount, open one to edit, clone it, or delete one or more at once.

Open **Appointments → Catalog → Discounts**.

![Discounts list](/img/catalog/discounts/listing.png)

## Add a discount

1. Click **Add New**.

See [Creating a new discount](./creating-a-new-discount).

## Search and filters

1. Click the search box above the table.
2. Type part of the discount name or a promo code.
3. Optionally filter by type, status (**Active** / **Disabled**), or date range.
4. Click **Reset filters** when you want every discount again.

## Row menu

1. Open the menu on a discount row.
2. Choose **Clone**, **Update**, or **Delete**.

![Row menu with Clone, Update, and Delete](/img/catalog/discounts/row-menu.png)

- **Clone** opens a new discount form filled from that discount so you can save a copy.
- **Update** opens the discount for editing. You can also click the discount name.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm, then removes the discount. See [Deleting a discount](./deleting-a-discount).

## Delete several discounts

1. Check the rows you want to remove, or use **Select all**.
2. Click **Delete selected**.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

This cannot be undone. Existing appointments that already used those discounts keep what they stored.

### Good to know

**Delete selected** stays disabled until at least one row is checked.
