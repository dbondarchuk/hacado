# Updating an existing discount

Change a discount when the code, value, dates, or limits no longer match your promotion. Edits apply the next time someone uses the discount. Past appointments keep what they already stored.

## Open the discount

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Discounts**.
2. Click the discount name, or open the row menu and choose **Update**.

![Discounts list](/img/catalog/discounts/listing.png)

The form shows the same sections as create: **Details**, **Dates**, **Maximum uses**, **Promo codes**, and **Limit to**.

## Change details

1. Update **Name** if you need a different unique name.
2. Switch **Status** between **Active** and **Disabled** to turn the promotion on or off without deleting it.
3. Change **Type** between **Percentage** and **Fixed amount** when the discount model changes.
4. Update **Value** to match the new type.

## Change dates and usage

1. Edit **Start date**, **End date**, **Earliest eligible appointment date**, or **Latest eligible appointment date**.
2. Clear a date field when that bound should no longer apply.
3. Update **Maximum total uses** or **Maximum uses per customer**, or clear them for no cap.

## Change promo codes and limits

1. Add, edit, or remove codes under **Promo codes** (still up to 10).

![Promo codes section](/img/catalog/discounts/promo-codes.png)

2. Add or remove **Limit to** cards, and change which services, add-ons, or packages each card includes. Multiple cards are **Or**; see the Limit to examples in create.

![Limit to section](/img/catalog/discounts/limit-to.png)

![Limit to packages](/img/catalog/discounts/limit-to-packages.png)

Full create-time detail for every field and Limit to rule is in [Creating a new discount](./creating-a-new-discount).

## Save

1. Click **Save**.

Hacado keeps you on the discount page and confirms that your changes were saved.

### Good to know

Set **Status** to **Disabled** when you want to pause a promotion without removing its codes or limits.
