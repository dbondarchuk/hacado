# Deleting an add-on

Delete an add-on when you no longer want that extra in the catalog. Deleting also removes the add-on from every service that used it. Past bookings keep whatever add-ons they already stored.

## Delete one add-on

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Add-ons**.
2. Open the row menu on the add-on.
3. Click **Delete**.

![Delete confirmation dialog](/img/catalog/addons/delete-confirm.png)

4. Read the confirm dialog. It asks **Are you sure?** and warns that the action cannot be undone.
5. Click **Continue** to delete, or **Cancel** to keep the add-on.

## Delete several add-ons

1. Check the add-ons to remove, or use **Select all**.
2. Click **Delete selected**.
3. Confirm in the dialog. That dialog also states that selected add-ons are removed from all services that use them.
4. Click **Delete selected** in the dialog, or **Cancel** to stop.

## What happens next

- The add-on leaves the **Add-ons** list.
- Hacado pulls the add-on off every [service](/docs/catalog/services) that referenced it, so new bookings no longer offer that extra there.
- You cannot undo a delete. Create the add-on again if you still need a similar extra.

### Good to know

If the list looks empty after a delete and you expected rows to remain, clear search - you may still be filtering the list.
