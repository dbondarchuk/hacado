# Updating an existing add-on

Change an add-on when the name, description, duration, price, fields, or staff rules no longer match how you book. Edits apply everywhere that add-on is already attached.

## Open the add-on

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Add-ons**.
2. Click the add-on name, or open the row menu and choose **Update**.

The edit form shows the current **Details**, **Duration and price**, **Fields**, and **Member overrides**.

## Change details

1. Update **Name** if you need a different unique name.
2. Edit **Short description** in the markdown editor when clients need clearer copy during booking.
3. Adjust **Duration** (hours and minutes) under **Duration and price**.
4. Adjust **Price**.

## Fields

1. Under **Fields**, click **Add a new item** to attach another [field](/docs/catalog/fields).
2. Open **Field** and pick the field.
3. Turn **Required** on or off for that field.
4. Remove a field with the trash control on its card, and confirm if asked.
5. Drag the grip to reorder fields.

## Member overrides

Use **Member overrides** when one staff member should use a different price or duration, or should not offer the add-on.

1. Click **Add override** if you need a new card.
2. Choose the **Member**.
3. Leave **Cannot offer this add-on** off, then set **Price override** and **Duration override** as needed.

![Member override with price and duration for a second staff member](/img/catalog/addons/member-overrides.png)

4. Turn **Cannot offer this add-on** on when that member must not offer the add-on. Price and duration overrides hide while it is on.

![Member override with Cannot offer this add-on enabled](/img/catalog/addons/member-unavailable.png)

5. To remove an override, click the trash control and confirm **Delete**, or **Cancel** to keep it.

## Save

1. Click **Save**.

Hacado keeps you on the add-on page and confirms that your changes were saved. Use **Discard** if you want to drop unsaved edits.

### Good to know

Changing an add-on updates every [service](/docs/catalog/services) that already uses it. Review those attachments if the price, duration, or questions change in a big way.
