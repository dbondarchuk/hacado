# Listing services

The **Services** list shows every service in your catalog. Use it to find a service, open one to edit, clone it, or delete one or more at once.

Open **Appointments → Catalog → Services**.

![Services list with name and updated columns](/img/catalog/services/listing.png)

## Add a service

The top-right control is a split button:

1. Click **Add New** to open a blank **New service** form.
2. Or open the chevron next to **Add New** and choose **Add from template**.

![Add New split button with Add from template](/img/catalog/services/add-split.png)

**Add from template** opens a dialog where you pick a profession, then a service template, then **Create**. The new form is prefilled so you can edit and save.

![Add from template dialog with profession and service picks](/img/catalog/services/add-from-template.png)

See [Creating a new service](./creating-a-new-service).

### Good to know

On the Free plan you can keep only one service. When you already have one, **Add New** and **Add from template** stay locked until you upgrade. See [Good to know](./good-to-know).

## Search

1. Click the search box above the table.
2. Type part of the service name.

The list updates to matching rows. Clear the box when you want every service again.

## Row menu

1. Open the menu on a service row.
2. Choose **Clone**, **Update**, or **Delete**.

![Row menu with Clone, Update, and Delete](/img/catalog/services/row-menu.png)

- **Clone** opens a new service form filled from that service so you can save a copy.
- **Update** opens the service for editing. You can also click the service name.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm, then removes the service. See [Deleting a service](./deleting-a-service).

## Delete several services

1. Check the rows you want to remove, or use **Select all**.
2. Click **Delete selected**.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

This cannot be undone. Hacado also removes those services from booking availability if they were listed there.

### Good to know

**Delete selected** stays disabled until at least one row is checked.
