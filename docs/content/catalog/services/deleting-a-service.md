# Deleting a service

Delete a service when you no longer offer it. Deleting removes it from Catalog and from booking availability if it was listed there. Past appointments keep the service details they already stored.

## Delete one service

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Services**.
2. Open the row menu on the service.
3. Click **Delete**.

![Row menu with Delete](/img/catalog/services/row-menu.png)

![Delete confirmation dialog](/img/catalog/services/delete-confirm.png)

4. Read the confirm dialog. It warns that the action cannot be undone.
5. Confirm to delete, or cancel to keep the service.

## Delete several services

1. Check the services to remove, or use **Select all**.
2. Click **Delete selected**.
3. Confirm in the dialog.
4. Click **Delete selected** in the dialog, or **Cancel** to stop.

## What happens next

- The service leaves the **Services** list.
- Hacado removes it from **Settings → Appointments → Services and packages** if it was available for booking.
- You cannot undo a delete. Create the service again if you still need a similar offering - see [Creating a new service](./creating-a-new-service).

### Good to know

If the list looks empty after a delete and you expected rows to remain, clear search - you may still be filtering the list.
