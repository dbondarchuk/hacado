# Overview

**Services** are the appointments clients book - for example a portrait session, a haircut, or a consultation. Each service has a name, length, price, and optional extras such as fields, add-ons, staff, deposits, and policies.

Open **Appointments → Catalog → Services** in the side menu.

A service lives in the catalog once. Creating or editing it here defines what the service is. Clients only see it on your booking page after it is added under **Settings → Appointments → Services and packages**.

For a lighter first-time pass, see [Set up your services](/docs/first-steps/set-up-your-services).

## What you can do here

- [Listing services](./services/listing-services) - search, open a row, clone, or delete one or more services.
- [Creating a new service](./services/creating-a-new-service) - start blank or from a template, set General options, and configure every tab.
- [Updating an existing service](./services/updating-an-existing-service) - change name, duration, price, staff, payments, and policies.
- [Deleting a service](./services/deleting-a-service) - remove a service from the catalog.
- [Good to know](./services/good-to-know) - Free plan limit, booking availability, and related Catalog links.

### Good to know

Saving a new service does not make it bookable by itself. After create, Hacado asks whether to add it to booking availability. You can also manage that later under [Services and packages](/docs/daily-use/appointment-settings-services-packages).
