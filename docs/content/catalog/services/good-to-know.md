# Good to know

These notes cover limits and links that cut across listing, creating, updating, and deleting services.

## Free plan limit

The Free plan includes one service. When you already have one, **Add New**, **Add from template**, and **Clone** stay unavailable until you upgrade to Solo (or higher). Edit or delete the existing service anytime; you just cannot keep a second one on Free.

## Creating is not the same as bookable

Saving a service only stores it in Catalog. Clients cannot book it until it is on **Settings → Appointments → Services and packages**.

After you create a service, Hacado asks **Add this service to booking availability?** Choose **Add to available services** to make it bookable now, or **Not now** to leave it in Catalog only. You can change that list later under [Services and packages](/docs/daily-use/appointment-settings-services-packages).

![Add this service to booking availability dialog](/img/catalog/services/availability-dialog.png)

Editing a service later does not show that dialog again and does not change availability by itself.

## Staff and booking

A service with no members assigned on the **Staff** tab is not available for booking. Assign at least one member. When two or more people can deliver the same service, add each member and use price or duration overrides as needed.
