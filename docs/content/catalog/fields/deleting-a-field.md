# Deleting a field

Delete a field when you no longer want that question in the catalog. Deleting also removes the field from every service and add-on that used it. Past bookings keep whatever answers they already stored.

## Delete one field

1. Open **Appointments → Catalog → Fields**.
2. Open the row menu on the field.
3. Click **Delete**.

![Delete confirmation dialog](/img/catalog/fields/delete-confirm.png)

4. Read the confirm dialog. It asks **Are you sure?** and warns that the action cannot be undone.
5. Click **Continue** to delete, or **Cancel** to keep the field.

## Delete several fields

1. Check the fields to remove, or use **Select all**.
2. Click **Delete selected**.
3. Confirm in the dialog. That dialog also states that selected fields are removed from all services and add-ons that use them.
4. Click **Delete selected** in the dialog, or **Cancel** to stop.

## What happens next

- The field leaves the **Fields** list.
- Hacado pulls the field off every service and add-on that referenced it, so new bookings no longer show that question there.
- You cannot undo a delete. Create the field again if you still need a similar question.

### Good to know

If the list looks empty after a delete and you expected rows to remain, clear search and the **Type** filter - you may still be filtering the list.
