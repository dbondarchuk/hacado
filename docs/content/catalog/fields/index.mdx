# Overview

**Fields** are questions you can attach to services and add-ons. Clients answer them while booking - for example a short note, a package choice, a policy checkbox, or a file upload.

Open **Appointments → Catalog → Fields** in the side menu.

A field lives in the catalog once. You reuse the same field on as many services or add-ons as you need. Changing the field updates it everywhere it is used.

## What you can do here

- [Listing fields](./fields/listing-fields) - search, filter by type, open a row, clone, or delete.
- [Creating a new field](./fields/creating-a-new-field) - add a field and choose its type.
- [Field types](./fields/field-types) - what each of the eight types asks the client for.
- [Updating an existing field](./fields/updating-an-existing-field) - change name, label, type, or options.
- [Deleting a field](./fields/deleting-a-field) - remove a field from the catalog and from services or add-ons that used it.

### Good to know

Creating a field does not put it on a booking yet. Attach it on the service or add-on you want clients to see it for.
