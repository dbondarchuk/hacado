# Services and packages (coming soon)

This guide will cover **Settings → Appointments → Services and packages**: which Catalog services and packages appear on your booking page.

Until it ships, know that creating a service in Catalog does not make it bookable by itself. After you create a service, Hacado may ask whether to add it to booking availability. You can also add or remove services and packages later under appointment settings.

For defining services, fields, add-ons, discounts, gift cards, and packages, see **[Catalog](/docs/catalog)**.
