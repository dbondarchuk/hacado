# Customer Appointment Updates

This App tells **customers** as soon as their booking changes. For each event (pending, confirmed, declined, canceled, no-show, rescheduled) you turn the update on and choose email, text, or both. Email uses your templates. Text uses built-in copy, or a custom template when your workspace allows it. You can also attach a calendar file to emails and use different emails for package appointments.

## Adding the App

1. Confirm outbound email (often **[SMTP](/docs/apps/smtp)**).
2. For texts, enable text messaging and add **[Textbelt SMS](/docs/apps/text-belt)** or your SMS option.
3. Open **Apps**, then **Store**, and install **Customer Appointment Updates**.
4. For each event, turn the update on. Pick an email template when email is on. Turn on text when you want SMS. Save.
5. Book a practice appointment and confirm the customer receives the right message.

Problems sending? **[Apps troubleshooting](/docs/apps/troubleshooting)**

### Good to know

**[Appointment Reminders](/docs/apps/appointment-reminders)** sends on a clock (for example, one day before). This App fires when the status changes. Choose both carefully so guests are not overloaded.

## Usage

### Confirm politely when you accept an appointment

**Use this when:** Clients need confidence the slot is theirs.

**You need:** A confirmed-stage email template and branding that match your voice.

### Text for urgent changes

**Use this when:** Customers respond faster to SMS than email.

**You need:** A phone number on the booking, consent where required, and SMS credits.

### Package-specific emails

**Use this when:** Package bookings should use different wording than standard appointments.

**You need:** Separate pending/confirmed package email templates turned on in this App.

## Removing the App

1. Open **Apps**, then **Installed apps**.
2. Remove **Customer Appointment Updates**.

### What changes afterward

Customers stop receiving these automatic status updates. Timed reminders from other Apps may still send.
