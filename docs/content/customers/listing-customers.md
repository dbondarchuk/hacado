# Listing customers

The **Customers** list shows everyone who has booked or been added manually. Use it to find a person, open their profile, merge duplicates, or remove records you no longer need.

Open **Customers** in the side menu.

![Customers list with name, email, phone, and appointment columns](/img/customers/listing.png)

## Columns

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Display name (click to open the profile) |
| **Email** | Primary email |
| **Phone** | Primary phone |
| **Last appointment** | Most recent past visit when one exists |
| **Upcoming appointment** | Next booked visit when one exists |
| **Appointments** | Count of appointments linked to this customer |

Rows can also show an avatar or initials.

## Search

1. Click the **Search...** box above the table.
2. Type part of a name, email, or phone.

The list updates to matching rows. Clear the box when you want everyone again.

## Add a customer

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Fill the form and save.

See [Creating a customer](./creating-a-customer).

## Open a customer

Click the customer **Name** (or choose **Edit** from the row menu) to open the profile on the **Details** tab. See [Working with a customer](./working-with-a-customer).

## Merge selected customers

Use merge when the same person appears more than once (for example they booked with a different email, then you added them by hand).

1. Check two or more customer rows.
2. Click **Merge selected**.
3. Choose the **Target customer** - the profile that remains.
4. Confirm **Merge selected**.

Hacado moves history onto the target and combines known names, emails, and phones where needed. The other selected profiles are removed.

## Delete selected customers

1. Check one or more rows.
2. Click **Delete selected**.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

You cannot delete a row that is already marked deleted. If delete fails because appointments are still linked, resolve or reassign those appointments first, then try again.

## Row menu

1. Open the menu on a customer row.
2. Choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

- **Edit** opens the customer profile.
- **Delete** asks for confirmation, then removes that customer.

### Good to know

- Merging is the safer fix for duplicates than deleting, because appointments and messages stay on one profile.
- Search matches primary contact fields and known alternate values when those are stored on the profile.
