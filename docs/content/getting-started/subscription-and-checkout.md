# Choose a plan

After you confirm your email and phone, Hacado opens **Choose your plan**. Every plan, including Free, continues to Polar checkout before setup.

## The plans

Each card shows the price, what is included, and a button:

- **Free** is $0 per month. Click **Start with Free**. It includes up to 15 appointments per billing cycle, one bookable service, a branded booking page, calendar sync, and email notifications. Text messages use SMS credits you buy separately.
- **Solo** is $29 per month and is marked **Recommended**. Click **Subscribe to Solo**. It adds unlimited bookings, services, and clients, online payments, your own domain, gift cards and discounts, packages, and 100 SMS credits each month.
- **Studio** is $59 per month and is marked **Good for business**. Click **Subscribe to Studio**. It is for a team: five members included, a calendar and schedule for each person, and 300 SMS credits each month.

Solo and Studio list what they add on top of the plan below them.

## Pay on Polar

1. Click the button on the plan you want.
2. Polar opens in the same window. Pick a payment method and fill in the billing address it asks for.
3. Check the total. Tax can be added from the billing address.
4. Click **Subscribe now**.

Hacado does not store your card. When Polar finishes, you return to [Set up your workspace](./workspace-install-wizard).

### Good to know

You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel later. Open **Settings**, then **Brand**, then **General**, and use **Manage plan** to open the Polar customer portal.
