# Get to know your dashboard

After setup, Hacado opens your **dashboard**. This is where you run the business day to day. The side menu takes you to other tools. The tabs on this screen keep the morning briefing in one place.

![Dashboard Overview with upcoming appointments](/img/first-steps/dashboard-overview.png)

## The side menu

The left menu groups everyday work. Click a label to open that area. Some rows open a short list when you click the arrow.

![Side menu with Overview, Appointments, Financials, Customers, Website, and Settings](/img/first-steps/dashboard-sidebar.png)

| Group | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The **Dashboard** home you are on now. |
| **Appointments** | Every booking, your weekly hours under **Schedule**, and bookable items under **Catalog**. |
| **Financials** | Money in and out. You can leave this for later. |
| **Customers** | People who have booked or that you added by hand. |
| **Website** | Public pages, images, and site appearance. |
| **Settings** | Brand, booking rules, messages, and apps. |

Your name at the bottom opens your personal account. **View website** at the top opens your public booking site.

## The tabs

On the dashboard home, three tabs sit under the greeting. Installed apps can add more tabs later; this tour covers the ones that are always there.

![Overview, Calendar, and Pending Appointments tabs](/img/first-steps/dashboard-tabs.png)

### Overview

**Overview** is your briefing for today and this week.

![Number cards for today and this week](/img/first-steps/dashboard-kpis.png)

- The number cards show today’s appointments, this week’s count, revenue, and average booking value.
- Quick links under the tabs jump to common places such as **Customers**, **Services**, and **Store**.

![Upcoming appointments list with pending and confirmed visits](/img/first-steps/dashboard-upcoming.png)

- **Upcoming appointments** lists the next visits. Click a row to open that booking.
- **This week** shows a small chart of how busy the week looks.
- **Needs attention** calls out work that waits on you, such as pending requests.

![Needs attention banner for pending appointment requests](/img/first-steps/dashboard-needs-attention.png)

Click **Review** on that banner to jump to the Pending Appointments tab.

### Calendar

**Calendar** shows the same bookings on a day, week, month, or agenda layout. Use **Today** and the arrows to move around. Click an appointment block to open it.

![Week calendar with portrait sessions booked](/img/first-steps/dashboard-calendar.png)

### Pending Appointments

**Pending Appointments** lists booking requests that still need your answer. Each card shows the service, client, time, and price. Use **Confirm** to accept or **Decline** to turn it down.

![Three pending portrait session cards with Confirm and Decline](/img/first-steps/dashboard-pending.png)

If you turn on **Auto-confirm bookings** in payments settings, new bookings skip this list and land as confirmed. You can still open any booking later under **Appointments**.
