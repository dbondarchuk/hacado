# Publish and share your booking link

A finished page is not live until it is **published**. Once your key pages are live, copy your booking link and share it where clients will find you.

## How publishing works

Each page goes live when you:

1. Open the page in **Website → Pages**.
2. Open the page settings while editing.
3. Turn on **Published**.
4. Set a **publish date** - use today or earlier so the page is visible now.
5. Tap **Save**.

Repeat for every page clients should see. Unpublished pages stay hidden from the public site.

The pages table shows **Published** status for each row. Your home page and booking-related pages should be published before you share your link widely.

![Pages list showing Published status](/img/first-steps/website-pages.png)

## Find your booking link

1. Open **Settings → Brand**.
2. Open the **Brand** tab.
3. Under **Your Hacado address**, use the **copy** button next to your website URL.

![Your Hacado address with copy and connect custom domain](/img/first-steps/settings-brand-address.png)

Open the link in a **private or incognito** browser window. What you see is close to what clients see.

## Preview vs live

- **Preview** (while editing) shows draft content with a special preview link - useful before you publish.
- **Live site** shows only **published** pages whose publish date has passed.

If something looks unfinished on the live link, return to **Website → Pages** or **Settings → Brand**.

## Use your own web address (optional)

You can keep your Hacado web address or point a domain you own at your site. That is optional for launch day.

When you are ready, follow **[Connect your own web address](/docs/daily-use/connect-domain)**.

## Share your link

Once you are happy with the live site, put your booking link where clients already look for you:

- Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn bio and posts
- Google Business Profile or other directory listings
- Email signature
- Printed cards or flyers
- A "Book now" button on an existing website
