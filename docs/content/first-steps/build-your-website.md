# Build your website

Your setup wizard already created starter pages for your booking site. Open the page list, confirm what is there, and edit only what you need for launch.

Open **Website → Pages** in the side menu.

![Pages list with Home, Book now, and other starter pages](/img/first-steps/website-pages.png)

## Understand your page list

You will see each page’s title, URL slug, and whether it is **Published**. Starter pages often include Home, Book now, About, and a service page.

Click a page title when you are ready to edit it in the page builder. For a full walkthrough of blocks, layouts, and the editor toolbar, see **[Page builder](/docs/website/page-builder)** (coming soon).

## Related website tools

- **Website → Assets** - upload photos and files you will use on pages.
- **Website → Appearance → Page headers** and **Page footers** - the top navigation and bottom section that repeat across pages.

## A sensible first pass

You do not need a perfect site on day one:

1. Open the home page and check the headline still sounds like you.
2. Confirm your services and booking pages are in the list.
3. Add a logo under **Settings → Brand** if you have not already.
4. Replace placeholder photos from **Assets** when you have real ones.

You can return later to add pages, forms, or a blog as your business grows.
