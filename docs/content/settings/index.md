# Settings overview

**Settings** is where you keep long-lived business choices: how your brand looks, who is on your team, how booking works, and how customers sign in or get texts.

Open **Settings** in the side menu, or go to **Dashboard → Settings**. You land on a hub of cards. Each card opens one settings area.

![Settings hub with Brand, Team, Appointments, Customer access, Text messaging, and more](/img/daily-use/settings/hub.png)

## Settings cards

| Card                                      | What it is for                                                                              | Guide                                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Brand**                                 | Business details, logo, website SEO, social links, styling, booking link, billing shortcuts | [Brand](./settings/brand)                     |
| **Team**                                  | Invite members, roles, and seats. Studio plan only. Free and Solo do not include this page  | [Team](./settings/team)                       |
| **Appointments**                          | Booking rules, deposits, cancellations, reschedules                                         | [Appointments](./settings/appointments)       |
| **Customer access**                       | How customers receive their one-time sign-in code                                           | [Customer access](./settings/customer-access) |
| **Text messaging**                        | Opt-in texts on booking and waitlist forms. Every plan. Custom templates are Solo and above | [Text messaging](./settings/text-messaging)   |
| **Templates**                             | Email and text wording for customer messages                                                | [Templates](/docs/communications/templates)   |
| **Logs**                                  | Sent and received message history                                                           | Under **Communications**                      |
| **Installed apps / Store / Default apps** | Connect calendars, payments, email, and more                                                | **[Apps](/docs/apps/overview)**               |

Related (not on the Settings hub, but next to booking):

- **[Default schedule](./settings/schedule)** — weekly hours under **Appointments → Schedule** in the side menu
- **[Services and packages](./settings/appointments-services-and-packages)** — which Catalog items clients can book (tab inside Appointment settings)

## How to save changes

Most Settings forms have a **Save** button at the bottom. Open a tab or card, make your edits, then **Save** before leaving. Unsaved changes are lost if you navigate away.

## Custom domain

Your public booking site starts on a Hacado address such as `https://yourname.hacado.me`. Connecting a name you buy yourself (for example `www.yourbusiness.com`) is optional and covered step by step in **[Connect your own domain](/docs/how-to-guides/domains/connect-your-own-domain)**.

## Good to know

- Outside tools (Stripe, Zoom, Google Calendar, and similar) live under **[Apps](/docs/apps/overview)**, not inside Brand tabs.
- Your personal password and profile are under your account menu, not the Settings hub.
