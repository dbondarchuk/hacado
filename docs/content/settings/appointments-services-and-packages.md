# Services and packages

Creating a service or package in **Catalog** does **not** make it bookable by itself. **Settings → Appointments → Services and packages** is where you choose what appears on the public booking flow.

Open **Settings → Appointments**, then the **Services and packages** tab.

![Services and packages with a Portraits group and Flexible Mini Session](/img/daily-use/settings/appointments-services.png)

## Before you start

- Create services, packages, fields, and add-ons under **[Catalog](/docs/catalog)**.
- Know which items should be live for clients right now (you can leave some in Catalog only).

## Add something clients can book

1. Click **Add new** (or the **+** on the Catalog card).
2. Choose **Add service**, **Add package**, or **Add group** depending on the menu.
3. Pick the Catalog item on the card.
4. Drag cards to set the order clients see.
5. Click **Save** on the appointment settings form.

A group needs a name. The short description is optional and appears under that name on the booking page. Use **Add service** or **Add package** on the group card to put items inside it. Items you leave outside a group are their own choices on the first step.

On the booking page, each group is one option. The client opens the group, then picks a service or package inside it. Order on this tab is the order on that page.

![Booking form service step with the Portraits group](/img/daily-use/settings/appointments-booking-options.png)

![Services inside the Portraits group on the booking form](/img/daily-use/settings/appointments-booking-group.png)

## Remove from booking (keep in Catalog)

1. Open the card’s delete control (trash).
2. Confirm removal from the list of available services.
3. **Save**.

The Catalog definition remains. Clients simply cannot book it until you add it again.

## After creating a service

When you save a new service in Catalog, Hacado may ask **Add this service to booking availability?** Choosing **Add to available services** puts it on this list immediately. Choosing **Not now** leaves it Catalog-only until you add it here.

See [Creating a new service](/docs/catalog/services/creating-a-new-service).

## Good to know

- Order on this tab is the order on the booking page.
- Empty groups show as empty until you add children.
- Per-service deposit, cancellation, and reschedule overrides still live on the service form; this tab only controls **availability**.
