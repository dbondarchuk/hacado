# Brand settings

**Settings → Brand** (page title **Site & brand**) holds how clients recognize your business online and on paperwork. Related items are split into four tabs along the top: **General**, **Brand**, **Social**, and **Styling**.

Open **Settings** in the side menu, then **Brand**. Or use the **Brand** card on the [Settings hub](./settings).

Click **Save** at the bottom after you finish a tab so Hacado stores your changes.

## General

Use **General** for information that should stay consistent across emails, your site, and search listings.

![General tab with business name, industry, contact details, timezone, country, and currency](/img/daily-use/settings/brand-general.png)

| Field | What it does |
| ----- | ------------ |
| **Business name** | Public name of your business. |
| **Industry** | Helps search and listings understand what you do. |
| **Business email** and **phone** | Contact details for the business. |
| **Time zone** | Clock used for scheduling and the booking page. |
| **Use customer's timezone** | On for mostly online work so clients see times in their zone. Off for in-person work so everyone sees your location’s clock. |
| **Country** and **Currency** | Shape how prices display at checkout. |
| **Street address** (and line 2, city, region, postal code) | Optional physical location on your site and in search. Use **Find address** for a lookup. |

### Subscription & billing

Further down the same tab, **Subscription & billing** shows your plan status, price, next billing date, team seats, and SMS credits. From here you can:

- **Open billing portal** to manage or change your plan
- **Purchase seats** when you need more team members
- **Buy SMS credits** for text messaging top-ups

![Subscription and billing card with plan, seats, and SMS credits](/img/daily-use/settings/brand-billing.png)

## Brand

Use the **Brand** tab for what visitors see on your booking website.

![Brand tab with visual identity, website fields, SEO, and Hacado address](/img/daily-use/settings/brand-brand.png)

### Visual identity

- **Logo** — click **Select** to pick an image from your assets. Shown in the site header and previews.

### Website

| Field | What it does |
| ----- | ------------ |
| **Site title** | Name on public pages and in the browser tab. |
| **Default site language** | Language clients read first. |
| **Favicon** | Square image for browser tabs (at least 512×512px PNG/JPEG/WebP/GIF, or SVG). |

### SEO

| Field | What it does |
| ----- | ------------ |
| **Meta description** | Short description for search engines. |
| **Keywords** | Words that help search engines understand your site. Add them as tags. |

### Your Hacado address

This card shows the public URL of your booking site and your organization slug.

![Your Hacado address with website URL and Connect custom domain](/img/daily-use/settings/brand-address.png)

- Use the **copy** button next to the website URL to share your booking link.
- **Connect custom domain** opens the wizard to use a name you buy yourself (for example `www.yourbusiness.com`). Full beginner walkthrough: **[Connect your own domain](/docs/how-to-guides/domains/connect-your-own-domain)**.
- Custom domains need a plan that includes them (Solo or higher). If you see an upgrade message, open billing from the General tab.
- **Update slug** is listed as coming soon.

## Social

Add links to profiles you want visitors to find (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and similar). Click **+** to add a link. These can appear on your public pages depending on your site layout.

![Empty social links list with add button](/img/daily-use/settings/brand-social.png)

For each link, pick a **Type** and paste the full **URL** to your profile. Drag to reorder. Remove a link when you no longer want it shown.

## Styling

Fine-tune **fonts** and **colour overrides** beyond what website design already set.

![Styling tab with fonts and colour overrides](/img/daily-use/settings/brand-styling.png)

1. Optionally set **Primary**, **Secondary**, and **Tertiary** fonts.
2. Under **Color overrides**, click **+** to replace a theme colour (background, text, card, and similar) with a HEX, rgb, or hsl value.
3. Optionally add **Additional CSS** for advanced tweaks.

Use these colours and fonts when you edit website pages so the look stays consistent.
